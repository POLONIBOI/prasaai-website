# What advertisers want to see before buying ads in a messaging/chat channel

**Context:** PRASA ai is an independent AI WhatsApp assistant for Cape Town Metrorail commuters (schedules, delay alerts, crowd reports). System Sol wants to sell in-chat advertising (sponsored line in a schedule reply; daypart-targeted offers) and needs an "Advertise with us" page + advertiser-facing audience data.

**Research question:** What do advertisers / media buyers need to SEE before they pay to run ads in a chat channel — which metrics, proof, formats, and benchmark numbers convince them?

---

## 1. The metrics advertisers require for a NEW channel

A media buyer evaluating an unproven channel scans for the same things in roughly this order: **audience (who + how many) → reach/impressions → engagement → cost efficiency (CPM/CPC/CPA) → measurement/attribution → brand safety.** A new channel has no track record, so the burden of proof is higher: you must substitute *transparency and a credible test offer* for history.

### Must-have metrics (the checklist a buyer runs)

| Metric | What it answers | What to publish for PRASA ai |
|---|---|---|
| **Audience size / subscriber base** | How many people can I reach? | Active users, monthly + daily (DAU/MAU) |
| **Reach** | Unique people exposed in a period | Weekly/monthly unique users who get a reply |
| **Impressions** | Total ad views (a sponsored line shown) | Messages-sent containing the ad slot |
| **Frequency** | Avg times one user sees the ad | Replies-per-user per week (commuters query daily → high) |
| **Engagement / CTR** | Do they act? | Tap-through on the sponsored link/button |
| **Conversion** | Did the click pay off? | Messaging-conversations-started, coupon redemptions, sign-ups |
| **CPM / CPC / CPA** | Cost efficiency vs. alternatives | Your rate card, benchmarked below |
| **Dayparting** | When is the audience live? | AM/PM peak heatmap (Section 5) |
| **Demographics + intent** | Right people, right mindset | LSM/income band, route/location, "high-intent: actively commuting" |
| **Brand safety** | Will my ad sit next to something bad? | E2E-encrypted, no UGC feed, opt-in only, frequency-capped, POPIA-compliant |
| **Viewability** | Was the ad actually seen? | Chat ads are ~100% viewable (single message in-thread, no scroll-past clutter) — a real selling point vs. display |
| **Measurement / attribution** | Can I prove ROI? | Unique promo codes, UTM links, click-to-WhatsApp handoff, redemption tracking |

**Key selling points unique to in-chat that buyers value:**
- **Viewability ~100%** — a sponsored line in a chat reply is read, unlike banner ads (display viewability often <60%).
- **Open rate ~98%** for WhatsApp messages vs. ~20% email — every served impression is a seen impression. ([Wapikit](https://www.wapikit.com/blog/whatsapp-marketing-stats-2024-insights), [Bird](https://bird.com/en-us/resources/guides/measure-whatsapp-performance-against-benchmarks))
- **Intent + context** — the user *asked* about their commute; an ad in that moment is contextually relevant (captive, attentive, location/time-specific).
- **First-party, opt-in audience** — strong brand-safety and POPIA story.

## 2. Benchmarks — messaging / conversational marketing

These are the numbers a buyer will compare your channel against. Conversational/WhatsApp engagement massively outperforms email and display, which is the core of the pitch.

| Metric | Benchmark | Source |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp message **open rate** | ~98% (some report ~70% for marketing broadcasts) | [Wapikit](https://www.wapikit.com/blog/whatsapp-marketing-stats-2024-insights), [Cooby](https://www.cooby.co/en/post/whatsapp-marketing-statistics) |
| WhatsApp **CTR** | 15–25% typical; e-comm 40–60%; best-in-class 50–80% | [Sendwo](https://sendwo.com/blog/whatsapp-click-through-rate-benchmarks-report/) |
| WhatsApp **conversion rate** | ~5% baseline; 18% segmented; 45–60% optimized conversational commerce | [Cooby](https://www.cooby.co/en/post/whatsapp-marketing-statistics), [YCloud](https://www.ycloud.com/blog/whatsapp-statistics-for-businesses) |
| SMS **open rate** (proxy) | ~98% | [Omnisend](https://www.omnisend.com/blog/sms-marketing-statistics/) |
| SMS **conversion** | 21–40% | [Sakari](https://sakari.io/blog/sms-marketing-benchmarks-2025-performance-metrics-and-industry-insights) |
| SMS/messaging **ROI** | up to $71 per $1 spent | [Omnisend](https://www.omnisend.com/blog/sms-marketing-statistics/) |

**Caveat for honesty in the kit:** the headline 98% open / 50%+ CTR figures come from *permission-based, personalized 1:1 marketing broadcasts*. A passive *sponsored line* inside an informational reply will see lower tap-through (realistically model 2–8% CTR). Present the high numbers as "channel potential / open-rate ceiling," and quote conservative CTR for the ad slot itself to stay credible.

### Cost of WhatsApp messaging (your underlying cost base)
Meta charges per template message (per-template model since July 2025). Marketing templates run **$0.025–$0.1365** per message depending on country; South Africa is a mid-tier band. Utility/service messages in the 24h window are cheaper or free. ([Gupshup](https://support.gupshup.io/hc/en-us/articles/38821010267673-WhatsApp-Pricing-Updates-2024-2025), [Meta dev docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing)) Managed WhatsApp programs cost SA businesses roughly **R2,500–R25,000/month**. ([GrowthPulse](https://www.growthpulsemedia.co.za/whatsapp-marketing-costs-south-africa/))

### Click-to-WhatsApp / Meta ads (relevant comparison)
- Global Meta **CPM** ~$11.82→$14.19; **CPC** ~$0.70–$0.78; **CPA** ~$38 (2026 blended). ([Get-Ryze](https://www.get-ryze.ai/blog/meta-ads-cost-benchmarks-by-industry-2026), [AdAmigo](https://www.adamigo.ai/blog/meta-ads-cpm-cpc-benchmarks-by-country-2026))
- **South Africa** Meta CPM band ~$5–$18 depending on objective/industry. ([Superads](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/south-africa)) → at ~R18.5/USD that's roughly **R90–R330 CPM**.
- Click-to-WhatsApp conversion metric is "Messaging Conversations Started"; first 72h of a CTWA-initiated convo is free. ([Respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads), [WOZTELL](https://support.woztell.com/portal/en/kb/articles/guide-to-click-to-whatsapp-with-meta-ads))

## 3. Benchmarks — OOH / transit advertising (how commuter audiences are valued)

Transit/OOH is the *closest conceptual comp* to PRASA ai (commuter audience, dayparting, captive attention) — and buyers already have transit budgets. Transit is the fastest-growing OOH category (+18.8% Q1 2024 spend; +7.3% in 2023). ([OAAA via Adsposure](https://www.adsposure.com/2024/05/13/transit-advertising-is-on-the-rise-heres-why/), [The Drum](https://www.thedrum.com/industry-insight/why-cpms-matter-and-how-ooh-advertising-is-winning-in-2025))

### US CPM by channel (2026 benchmark — low / median / high) ([Adsposure](https://www.adsposure.com/cpm/))

| Channel | CPM (USD) |
|---|---|
| **Bus advertising** | $1 / 3 / 7 |
| **Transit street furniture** | $2 / 5 / 15 |
| **Bulletin billboards** | $3 / 5 / 10 |
| **Poster billboards** | $3 / 5 / 9 |
| **Digital place-based / airport** | $9 / 18 / 32 |
| **Programmatic DOOH** | $5 / 8 / 15 |
| Terrestrial radio | $4 / 7 / 12 |
| Social media | $5 / 12 / 45 |
| Mobile display | $1 / 2.50 / 7 |
| Broadcast TV primetime | $35 / 45 / 60 |
| Podcasts (blended) | $18 / 28 / 50 |

**Takeaway:** transit OOH is among the *cheapest* CPMs ($2–$10), but it is **unmeasured, non-clickable, low-attention, and not addressable.** PRASA ai can price *above* static transit CPM because it is clickable, measurable, 1:1, time-targeted, and ~100% viewable — while still pricing *below* premium digital. That gap is your pricing argument.

**Commuter audience value props (validated):** average city commuter sees 5,000+ OOH ads/month; OOH exposure peaks **7–9AM and 4–7PM**; 62% of US jobs are within 0.5mi of a transit stop. ([Marketing LTB](https://marketingltb.com/blog/statistics/out-of-home-ooh-advertising-statistics/), [Broadsign](https://broadsign.com/blog/transit-advertising-how-to-reach-audiences-on-the-move-with-out-of-home/))

### South African OOH / transit context
- SA billboards: **R14,000–R350,000/month** by size/location. ([Pinpoint Media](https://ppmedia.co.za/billboard-advertising-south-africa/))
- Transit.TV reaches a **captive 13.2M viewers/month**; Cape Town MyCiTi BRT moves **4M+ passengers/month**; Transit Ads holds MyCiTi exclusive rights. ([In Touch Media](http://www.intouchmedia.co.za/transit-advertising-commuter-markets/), [Transit Ads](https://www.transitads.co.za/))
- The commuter market drives SA retail growth — commuters are a defined, high-value FMCG/retail/telco target. ([Transit Ads](https://www.transitads.co.za/category/transit-ads/))

## 4. South African digital ad market + WhatsApp context (for the pitch)

- SA digital ad market: **~$7.0bn (2024)**, growing **17.3% CAGR** to ~$18.4bn by 2030; mobile advertising **>R5bn**. ([Grand View](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/digital-advertising-market/south-africa), [Meltwater](https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/2025-social-media-statistics-south-africa))
- **WhatsApp dominance in SA:** 93.8% of active internet users use WhatsApp; favourite app for 34% (#1, ahead of TikTok/Facebook); ~23M+ users; near-96% messaging penetration. ([Meltwater](https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/2025-social-media-statistics-south-africa), [Cooby](https://www.cooby.co/en/post/whatsapp-marketing-statistics))
- Engagement: SA users spend **~23h42m/month on WhatsApp**; 50.8M internet users (78.9%); 99.3% of them on smartphones. ([Meltwater](https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/2025-social-media-statistics-south-africa))
- WhatsApp conversational commerce in SA: **45–60% conversion**, up to 12× traditional channels. ([YCloud](https://www.ycloud.com/blog/whatsapp-statistics-for-businesses))
- **MoyaApp** proves the SA data-free model works: 6M active users, 4M daily. Reinforces "reach low-data commuters where they already are." ([News24](https://www.news24.com/news24/bi-archive/the-moyaapp-has-6m-active-users-and-challenging-whatsapp-2022-8))

### Cape Town Metrorail audience size (use for reach tiles)
- Western Cape Metrorail: **22.7M passengers (FY2025)**; daily trips grew **53,000 (Apr 2024) → 104,000 (2025)**, targeting **162,400** this year and **343,000 by Apr 2026**. ([Wikipedia: Metrorail Western Cape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrorail_Western_Cape), [Stacker News](https://stacker.news/items/993505))
- PRASA national Metrorail: **77M trips FY2025**. ([Wikipedia: Metrorail (SA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrorail_(South_Africa)))

**Implication:** the *addressable* commuter universe is hundreds of thousands daily and growing fast — a credible "early-mover, growing audience" story. Quote PRASA ai's *own* user numbers as the hero stat; use Metrorail ridership as the total-addressable-market backdrop.

## 5. Relevant verticals + commuter ad specifics

**Highest-fit advertiser verticals** (commuter intent + SA market):
- **Retail / FMCG** — commuters are a proven SA retail driver; daypart grocery/QSR offers to PM peak ("grab dinner on the way home").
- **Telco / data** — airtime, data bundles, data-free offers (massively relevant to this audience).
- **Fintech / banking** — wallets, buy-now-pay-later, transport payments, insurance.
- **QSR / coffee** — AM-peak coffee/breakfast, PM-peak meal deals near stations.
- **Government / PSAs / NGOs** — safety, health, civic messaging to a hard-to-reach commuter audience (a differentiated, non-controversial revenue line).
- **Events / education / retail-media** — near-station promotions, route-specific store offers.

**Commuter ad mechanics buyers care about:**
- **Dayparting** — AM peak (~6–9AM) and PM peak (~4–7PM) are premium; price them higher.
- **Captive, high-intent audience** — the user opened the app *because* they're commuting; attention is high, mindset is relevant.
- **Location / route targeting** — Northern vs Southern vs Central line; near-CBD vs township corridors → targetable offers (e.g., a store at a specific station).
- **Context targeting** — a delay-alert reply can carry a "while you wait" offer; a schedule reply can carry a daypart offer.

## 6. What an effective media kit / "Advertise with us" page contains

Buyers scan a kit in a predictable order: **who's the audience → how big/engaged → what can I run → what does it cost → does it work → how do I start.** Standard high-converting components ([InfluenceFlow](https://influenceflow.io/resources/media-kit-the-complete-guide-to-showcasing-your-audience-demographics-and-engagement-rates/), [Wonderslide](https://wonderslide.com/blog/a-comprehensive-guide-to-a-great-media-kit/)):

1. **One-line value prop** — "Reach Cape Town's daily rail commuters the moment they plan their trip."
2. **Audience profile** — size (DAU/MAU), demographics (age, LSM/income, gender), location/route split, intent ("actively commuting, daily").
3. **Reach & engagement stats** — users, messages/replies per day, open rate, sessions per user.
4. **Ad formats** — visual mockups of each placement (in-chat sponsored line, daypart offer card, delay-alert sponsorship).
5. **Dayparting / "best time to advertise"** — the peak/off-peak heatmap (Section 7).
6. **Rate card** — formats, CPM and/or flat weekly/monthly, peak vs off-peak premium, minimum spend.
7. **Brand safety & compliance** — opt-in, POPIA, frequency caps, content policy, no offensive adjacency.
8. **Measurement / attribution** — promo codes, click tracking, reporting cadence.
9. **Case study / pilot results** — even one pilot's CTR/redemptions; offer a discounted **launch pilot** to manufacture proof.
10. **Clear CTA + contact** — "Book a placement" form, email, downloadable PDF rate card.

## What to put on the Advertise page & advertiser data

### A. Audience-stat tiles (hero band — pick 6–8; fill with PRASA ai's real numbers)

| Tile | Example value | Backing |
|---|---|---|
| Daily active commuters | **[X,000] / day** | PRASA ai analytics (hero stat) |
| Messages / replies served per day | **[XX,000]** | = impression inventory |
| Message open rate | **~98%** | WhatsApp benchmark |
| Audience that's mobile-first | **99% on smartphone** | Meltwater SA 2025 |
| Avg sessions per user / week | **[5–10]** (daily commute → repeat) | own data |
| Addressable commuter market | **104,000+ daily rail trips, → 343,000 by 2026** | Metrorail WC |
| Audience location | **Northern / Central / Southern line split** | own data |
| Engagement | **~24h/month on WhatsApp (SA avg)** | Meltwater SA 2025 |
| Brand-safe | **Opt-in • POPIA-compliant • encrypted** | platform fact |

> Lead with **your own DAU and daily-messages numbers** — those are the real inventory. Use Metrorail's 104k→343k ridership as TAM context, not as your reach.

### B. Ad-format mockups (show, don't tell)

**Format 1 — In-chat sponsored line** (inside a schedule reply):
```
🚆 Northern Line — Bellville → Cape Town
Next train: 07:42  (Platform 2)
Following:  08:05, 08:24

— Sponsored —
☕ Vida e Caffè @ Cape Town Station — R10 off
   your morning coffee. Show this chat.  [Get offer →]
```

**Format 2 — Daypart offer card** (PM peak, contextual):
```
🚆 PM Peak • Southern Line running on time

— Sponsored —
🛒 Heading home? Checkers Sixty60 — R50 off
   your first order. Tap to claim.  [Claim →]
```

**Format 3 — Delay-alert sponsorship** ("while you wait"):
```
⚠️ Central Line delay: ~15 min on your route.

Brought to you by [Brand]:
While you wait — [offer / PSA].  [Learn more →]
```
Design rules for credibility: always label **"Sponsored,"** max **one ad per reply**, frequency-capped, never inside a safety-critical line, clear single CTA button.

### C. Example rate card (ZAR — illustrative, anchor to your real reach)

Pricing logic: sit **above static transit CPM** (clickable + measurable + 100% viewable) but **below premium social/CTWA CPM.** SA Meta CPM ≈ R90–R330; transit OOH CPM ≈ R40–R190 equivalent. Target **CPM ~R120–R250**, peak commands a premium.

| Placement | Format | Daypart | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **In-chat sponsored line** | text + link | Off-peak | **R150 CPM** |
| **In-chat sponsored line** | text + link | AM/PM peak | **R250 CPM** |
| **Daypart offer card** | rich card + CTA | Peak | **R300 CPM** or **R8,000 / week** flat |
| **Delay-alert sponsorship** | contextual | All-day, capped | **R12,000 / week** (category-exclusive) |
| **Route/line takeover** | one line, all replies | Peak, 2 wks | **R20,000 / 2 weeks** |
| **PSA / government rate** | text | Off-peak | **R90 CPM** (discounted) |

- **Minimum spend:** R5,000 / campaign.  **Launch-pilot offer:** 50% off first 2 weeks to generate a case study.
- Offer both **CPM** (familiar to digital buyers) and **flat weekly** (familiar to OOH buyers) — let them choose.

### D. Peak / off-peak dayparting model (for the heatmap)

Commuter rail traffic is sharply bimodal. Model **relative reply-volume index (peak = 100)** by hour × weekday. Use this to drive a color heatmap and to justify the peak premium.

**Hourly volume index (weekday, peak = 100):**

| Hour | Index | Band |
|---|---|---|
| 05:00–06:00 | 35 | Shoulder |
| **06:00–07:00** | **85** | **AM peak** |
| **07:00–08:00** | **100** | **AM peak (max)** |
| **08:00–09:00** | **90** | **AM peak** |
| 09:00–11:00 | 40 | Off-peak |
| 11:00–14:00 | 30 | Off-peak (midday lull) |
| 14:00–16:00 | 45 | Shoulder (rising) |
| **16:00–17:00** | **80** | **PM peak** |
| **17:00–18:00** | **95** | **PM peak (max)** |
| **18:00–19:00** | **75** | **PM peak** |
| 19:00–21:00 | 35 | Evening tail |
| 21:00–05:00 | 10 | Overnight |

**Day-of-week multiplier:**

| Day | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Mon | 1.05 |
| Tue–Thu | 1.00 |
| Fri | 0.95 |
| Sat | 0.55 |
| Sun / public holiday | 0.35 |

**How to present it to advertisers:**
- A 7×24 (day × hour) heatmap, hot cells = AM 07:00 and PM 17:00 on weekdays.
- Two clear premium windows: **AM peak 06:00–09:00**, **PM peak 16:00–19:00** ("when your audience is captive and planning their day").
- Frame off-peak/midday + weekends as **value-rate inventory** (lower CPM) — gives budget buyers an entry point and fills inventory.
- Tie verticals to dayparts: **AM** = coffee/breakfast/news/data bundles; **PM** = QSR/grocery/retail/entertainment; **midday** = services/fintech/PSAs.

> Validate the index against PRASA ai's actual hourly message logs before publishing — the *shape* (bimodal AM/PM peaks) is correct for commuter rail; tune the magnitudes to real data.
